1. Elements must meet minimum color contrast ratio thresholds

106 instance(s) · WCAG 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) (Level AA)

Text is too faint against its background. People with low vision, older visitors and anyone on a phone in sunlight may not be able to read it. WCAG AA requires a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text.

How to fix: Darken the text or lighten the background until the ratio reaches 4.5:1 (3:1 for text 24px+ or 18.66px+ bold). The exact colours and measured ratio are listed per element below; a free checker such as WebAIM's contrast checker shows a passing shade.

2. Keyboard focus is not visible

84 instance(s) · WCAG 2.4.7 Focus Visible (Level AA)

When a keyboard user tabs onto these elements nothing visibly changes, so they cannot see where they are on the page.

How to fix: Do not remove the browser outline. Add a clear focus style, e.g. a:focus-visible, button:focus-visible, input:focus-visible { outline: 3px solid #1a5fb4; outline-offset: 2px; }.

What this report cannot tell you

Automated testing reliably catches a large share of common, objectively testable problems (missing alt text, missing labels, contrast, missing names, language, some structure) — industry data puts it at roughly half of real-world issues by volume — but it cannot judge:

  • whether alt text and link text are meaningful (only whether they exist);
  • whether the reading and focus order make sense, or whether every task can be completed by keyboard (we test Tab movement, not every interaction);
  • how the site behaves with real screen readers (JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver) and voice control;
  • captions and transcripts for video/audio content, or the accessibility of PDFs and third-party widgets inside iframes (booking, chat, payments);
  • error handling and instructions in multi-step forms and checkout;
  • pages behind a login, or states that only appear after interaction (menus, modals, validation messages).

A clean automated result is therefore not evidence of WCAG conformance. For a full conformance assessment, combine these fixes with manual testing by a person using assistive technology.
