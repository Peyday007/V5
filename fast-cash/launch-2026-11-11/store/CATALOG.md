# Catalog: "Exactly One Solution" puzzle gift shop

Platform: **Fourthwall Free plan** (see `COST.md` for why). All 10 products are print-on-demand blanks from the Fourthwall catalog. Nothing is stocked. Fourthwall's production partner (Printful for 9 products, Spoke for the journal) prints and ships each order.

Every grid on every product is a real sudoku with exactly one solution:

- It was generated by Brain's puzzle kernel (`server/services/puzzle/formats/sudoku.ts`).
- Brain's own validator proved it (all six checks pass, including "exactly one solution" and "the printed solution is that solution").
- A separate brute-force solver then re-checked it and found exactly one solution, matching the printed answer.

Each puzzle's seed and canonical hash are in `designs/_source/puzzles.json`, so anyone can re-render and re-check any grid.

Prices were read on **2026-11-11** from the public Fourthwall catalog (`https://products.fourthwall.com/<slug>`, in each page's embedded product data). Each page states the US shipping rate and transit time (7–9 or 8–10 days). On Fourthwall **the customer pays shipping at checkout** at the carrier's cost ([fourthwall.com/faq](https://www.fourthwall.com/faq), read 2026-11-11). Shipping is therefore not deducted from your profit, unless you run a free-shipping promo.

**Profit per unit** = retail − Fourthwall base cost − card processing.

- Card processing is **2.9% + $0.30** on US cards ([fourthwall.com/pricing](https://fourthwall.com/pricing), read 2026-11-11). It is computed here on retail + shipping, which slightly overstates the fee.
- Fourthwall takes **no extra percentage** on catalog products: the base cost *is* its flat fee.
- Sales tax is added on top at checkout and remitted by Fourthwall as merchant of record, so it is not your money and not in these numbers.

## Files
- `designs/<product>/`: each print file (300 DPI tagged; transparent RGBA for apparel and tote) plus `mockup-preview*.png`. The mockup is a rough 1200×1200 preview for social posts. On the store, use Fourthwall's own generated mockups.
- `designs/_source/`: `puzzles.json` (seeds, hashes, grids, solutions, validator checks), `generate-puzzles.ts`, `render.cjs` and `mockup.cjs`, which re-create every file.
- `catalog.csv`: one row per product: blank, catalog URL, variants, colours, base cost, shipping, retail, fee, profit, margin, SEO title, meta description, description, tags, files, puzzle seed.
