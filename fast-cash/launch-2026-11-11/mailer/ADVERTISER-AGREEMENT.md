## 1. What the Advertiser buys

1. **Ad space** on a 9" × 12" full-colour postcard ("the Card"). The space is the
   one named on the order: Standard (2.8" × 2.33"), Double (5.7" × 2.33"),
   Back Premium or Front Feature (each 5.7" × 2.33").
2. **Mailing** by USPS Every Door Direct Mail (EDDM) Retail to the residential
   addresses on the carrier routes named on the order:
   - Zone 1: Camp Hill & East Hampden, ZIP 17011, 11 routes, 4,944 homes by
     USPS count on 2026-11-11.
   - Zone 2 (if ordered): Hampden Township West, ZIP 17050, 7 routes, 4,950 homes.

   USPS route counts change slightly over time. The Card is mailed to every
   residential address USPS counts on those routes on the mailing date.
3. **Planned mailing date:** Monday, January 4, 2027, at the post office that
   serves the routes. USPS sets no delivery standard for EDDM. Publisher's
   estimate is that the Card reaches homes about January 5–12, 2027, but USPS
   does not guarantee a delivery date and Publisher does not either.
4. **Ad design included.** Publisher builds the ad from the Advertiser's logo,
   offer and contact details. The Advertiser may instead supply print-ready art
   at the exact size, 300 dpi, CMYK, as PDF or PNG.

## 2. Price and payment

1. The price is the one on the order. Founding prices for Zone 1: Standard
   $295, Double $525, Back Premium $575, Front Feature $625. Both zones:
   $495 / $895 / $975 / $1,050.
2. **Payment is due in full when the order is placed**, by the Stripe payment
   link or invoice Publisher sends (card or US bank account). A space is
   reserved only when it is paid.
3. No sales tax is charged. Publisher sells advertising space on direct mail
   delivered by USPS.
4. **Founding renewal.** If the Advertiser renews before each next edition's
   sell-by date, its Founding price is held for the next two editions.

## 3. Category exclusivity

1. Publisher accepts **one advertiser per category per zone**. The Advertiser's
   category is the one written on the order (for example "general / family
   dentistry"). Publisher decides close calls in good faith. For example,
   "orthodontics" and "general dentistry" are separate categories. Publisher
   tells the Advertiser before payment if a category is close to one already
   sold.
2. Exclusivity goes to the business that **pays** first, not the first to ask.

## 4. The minimum and the refund guarantee

1. **The Card prints only if at least 10 of its 22 ad cells are paid by
   Wednesday, December 9, 2026, at 5:00 pm Eastern** ("the Threshold"). A
   Standard space is 1 cell. Double, Back Premium and Front Feature spaces are
   2 cells each.
2. **If the Threshold is not met, Publisher refunds 100% of every payment by
   Friday, December 11, 2026** (within 2 business days), to the original payment
   method. Publisher absorbs the processing fees.
3. Zone 2 has its own Threshold of 10 cells, where both-zone orders count. If
   Zone 2 misses it, a both-zone Advertiser is refunded the difference between
   its both-zone price and the Zone 1 price, and its ad runs in Zone 1.
4. Publisher will not order printing or buy postage before the Threshold is met.
   Publisher will tell every Advertiser by email on December 9 whether the Card
   is printing.

## 5. Ad content, proof and approval

1. **Content due:** within 3 business days of payment, and no later than
   Wednesday, December 9, 2026, at 5:00 pm. Content means logo, offer text,
   phone, website or address, and any required licence or registration number.
   If no content arrives, Publisher builds the ad from the Advertiser's public
   logo and listing and sends it as the proof.
2. **Proof:** Publisher sends a proof by email within 1 business day of
   receiving content. The price includes one round of changes.
3. **Approval deadline:** Thursday, December 10, 2026, at 12:00 noon Eastern. If
   the Advertiser has not replied by then, the last proof sent is treated as
   approved.
4. The Advertiser is responsible for checking spelling, numbers, prices, dates
   and contact details on the approved proof.

## 6. Advertiser's warranties (truth in advertising)

The Advertiser warrants that:

1. Every statement in its ad is **truthful, not misleading, and backed by
   evidence**, as the FTC Act and Pennsylvania's Unfair Trade Practices and
   Consumer Protection Law require. Offers will be honoured as printed, for the
   stated period.
2. It has the right to use every logo, image, trademark and name in its ad.
3. The ad meets every rule for its profession or trade. Examples:
   - a home-improvement contractor shows its PA Home Improvement Contractor
     registration number;
   - a real-estate licensee shows its broker's name and office phone as the
     State Real Estate Commission requires;
   - licensed health professionals follow their board's advertising rules.
4. The ad contains no lottery, raffle, sweepstakes requiring purchase or other
   gambling offer, and nothing obscene. Such content is not mailable under
   federal law.
5. The Advertiser will hold Publisher harmless from claims arising from the
   Advertiser's ad content or offer.

Publisher may refuse or ask for changes to any ad it reasonably believes breaks
these warranties or USPS rules. If Publisher refuses an ad and no acceptable
version is agreed by the approval deadline, Publisher refunds the Advertiser in
full.

## 7. Publisher's commitments

1. Print the Card at full colour on 14 pt stock or better, at the space size
   ordered.
2. Mail the Card by USPS EDDM Retail to all the residential addresses on the
   ordered routes, with the USPS EDDM Retail indicia.
3. Send the Advertiser a photo of the post-office drop-off and of the USPS EDDM
   payment receipt showing the route list and piece count.
4. If the Card misses the planned January 4, 2027 mailing date by more than
   7 days for a reason within Publisher's control, the Advertiser may choose a
   full refund instead.

## 8. Limits

1. USPS delivers the Card. Publisher is not liable for USPS delays or
   non-delivery of individual pieces.
2. Publisher's total liability under this agreement is limited to the amount
   the Advertiser paid.
3. Publisher does not guarantee any number of responses, calls or sales.
4. If a printing error by Publisher or its printer makes the Advertiser's ad
   materially wrong against the approved proof, Publisher's sole remedy is a
   refund of that ad's price or a free equal space in the next edition, at the
   Advertiser's choice.

## 9. General

Pennsylvania law governs this agreement. It is the whole agreement for this
edition. Paying the order link or invoice, or replying "I agree" to the order
email, accepts these terms.
