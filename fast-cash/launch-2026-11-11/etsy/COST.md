# Etsy launch: what it costs and what each sale nets

Everything was read on **2026-11-11**. Figures quoted from Etsy's Help Center are marked **[Etsy]**.
Figures from other sources are marked **[3rd party]**. Figures I worked out are marked **[estimate]**.
I could not read etsy.com from this network: `www.etsy.com/legal/fees` returned **HTTP 403**. Etsy's
Help Center (`help.etsy.com`) and community forum did load, so the Etsy figures below come from there.

## 1. Cash needed to open the shop and publish all 8 listings

| Item | Amount | Source |
|---|---|---|
| One-time shop set-up fee | **Most likely $15** (amount shown before you pay; reports range up to about $30) | see note A |
| Listing fee, 8 listings × $0.20 | **$1.60** | [Etsy] "There is a $0.20 fee for creating or renewing a listing on Etsy." https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000344908 |
| Etsy Ads | **$0**. Leave them off. | optional |
| Monthly fee | $0 on the standard plan | no subscription is needed |
| **Total to activate** | **≈ $16.60** (up to about $31.60 if Etsy asks for the higher set-up fee) | |

Payment for these is taken from the debit or credit card you add during setup. Etsy says you "Add a
debit or credit card to pay Etsy bills" (https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015672808).

**Note A — set-up fee.** Etsy's own pages do not state the amount any more.
- [Etsy] The setup guide says "a one-time, non-refundable shop set-up fee must be paid", and you
  authorize it under "Total due now" (https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015672808).
- [Etsy] The forum announcement says "This fee applies to most new shops" and "the amount due will be
  shown before completing final shop set-up steps"
  (https://community.etsy.com/forum/announcements-290/topic/updated-624-strengthening-new-shop-onboarding-to-keep-our-community-safe-22387/).
- [3rd party] Etsy's fee policy first said "a one-time set-up fee of $15 USD". It was changed on
  2024-06-20 to "you may be required to pay a one-time set-up fee", and some sellers were then charged
  $28.50–$30 (https://valueaddedresource.net/etsy-variable-shop-setup-fee).
- [3rd party] eRank describes it as a "$15-$29 setup fee" (https://help.erank.com/blog/understanding-etsys-new-15-setup-fee/).

So budget **$15**, and keep **up to $30** available. You will see the exact figure before you agree to pay it.

## 2. Fees on each sale (US seller)

| Fee | Rate | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Transaction fee | **6.5%** of the order total | [Etsy] "Etsy charges 6.5% of the total order amount in your designated listing currency." https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035902374 |
| Payment processing (US bank account) | **3% + $0.25** per order | [Etsy] US row "3% + 0.25 USD". The fee is "taken from the item's total sale price, including its shipping fees, and any applicable sales tax." https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015628847 |
| Auto-renew on sale (multi-quantity listing) | **$0.20** per unit sold | [Etsy] "We only charge you $0.20 each for the extra quantity you sell." https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000344908 |
| Offsite Ads, only when a sale is attributed to an Etsy ad off Etsy | **15%** of that order (12% after a shop reaches $10k/yr) | [Etsy] "All sellers are automatically enrolled". Shops under $10,000 can opt out. https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000338367 |
| Etsy Ads (on Etsy, pay per click) | $0 if left off | optional |

### Net per sale [estimate]

This assumes a buyer charged about 6% sales tax, because processing is charged on the tax too, and no Offsite Ad.

| Price | Transaction 6.5% | Processing 3%×(1.06·P)+$0.25 | Renewal | **Total fees** | **You keep** | You keep if an Offsite Ad was credited (−15%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $3.49 | $0.23 | $0.36 | $0.20 | $0.79 | **$2.70** | $2.18 |
| $3.99 | $0.26 | $0.38 | $0.20 | $0.84 | **$3.15** | $2.56 |
| $4.49 | $0.29 | $0.39 | $0.20 | $0.88 | **$3.61** | $2.93 |
| $9.99 | $0.65 | $0.57 | $0.20 | $1.42 | **$8.57** | $7.07 |

- **Each sale costs nothing to fulfil.** Etsy delivers the files, and nothing is printed or shipped.
- **Break-even [estimate].** About 5–6 sales at $3.49–$3.99 pay back a $15 set-up fee plus $1.60 in
  listings. About 2 bundle sales do the same.
- **Sales tax.** Etsy charges the buyer and collects it. It is not a cost to you, but it raises the
  base that the 3% processing fee is charged on, and the table includes that.

## 3. When the money arrives

- [Etsy] **Digital sales are not held back.** "Sales of digital items are not held in reserve."
  New shops can have a payment reserve placed on physical items, but not on these.
  https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360058722214
- [Etsy] **Deposits are weekly to start.** "If you're a new seller, by default your funds will be sent
  to your bank account weekly, every Monday." You can change the schedule in Shop Manager › Finances ›
  Payment settings. A daily deposit needs at least USD 25.00, and changing your bank details puts a
  5-day hold on funds. https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360046998234

## 4. Competitor prices, for context [3rd party: search-result snippets read 2026-11-11]

Etsy listing pages returned 403 here, so these prices come from search snippets. Prices change and
some were sale prices.

- Large-print sudoku PDFs on Etsy: about **$2.90–$4.25**. Examples: "1000 Sudoku … Large Print, PDF"
  at $2.90 and "Extra Large Print Sudoku Puzzle Book for Adults" at $2.99
  (https://www.etsy.com/listing/1820174843, https://www.etsy.com/listing/4394745133,
  https://www.etsy.com/listing/1854467775).
- Christmas large-print word search PDFs: £2.09 on sale (from £2.80), and $2.99 on sale (from $4.99)
  (https://www.etsy.com/listing/4452104530, https://www.etsy.com/listing/1455820293).
- Outside Etsy: Getly sells "100 large print sudoku" at $6.99 and a 50-puzzle large-print
  Halloween/Christmas word search at $6.99
  (https://www.getly.store/product/large-print-sudoku-for-adults-100-puzzles-easy-to-expert-with-answers,
  https://www.getly.store/product/large-print-word-search-50-themed-puzzles-halloween-christmas-pdf).
  A Gumroad Christmas large-print book sells at $5.99 (https://cultradigital.gumroad.com/l/nwdmzr).
- Free competition exists: daily-sudoku.com gives away large-print sudoku PDFs
  (https://daily-sudoku.com/printable-sudoku/large-print). A paid product has to win on themes,
  bundling, a gift-ready cover and guaranteed one-solution puzzles.

**Pricing decision.** Single books are **$3.49–$4.49**, in the middle and upper part of the Etsy range.
That price is backed by one puzzle per page, festive covers, proved puzzles and solutions. The bundle
is **$9.99**. Bought separately, its five books cost $20.45, which gives the "less than half price"
claim in its listing. Run a **20–25% off** shop sale in launch week rather than cutting the list price
(see SHOP-SETUP.md › After launch).
