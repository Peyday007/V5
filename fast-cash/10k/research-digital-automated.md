# Automated digital products and AI-delivered services — settled cash by 2026-11-27

Research date: 2026-11-02/03. Window: about 24 days. Owner rules: no physical work; everything sold, delivered and managed online; ≤$300 at risk before income, ≤$3,000 total; no new paid APIs (Claude subscriptions plus the free puzzle/PDF generator are the tools); paid ads within the caps are allowed; no Upwork/Fiverr bidding as the model.

Labels: **PUBLISHED** means stated on the cited page (vendor pages are vendor claims, and forum posts are anecdotes). **ESTIMATE** means my own reasoning. "Settled" means money in a bank account, or withdrawable to one, by Friday 11-27. Thanksgiving is Thursday 11-26, a US bank holiday.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **Settlement, not demand, sets the deadline.** Every new processor or marketplace account holds the *first* money:
   - Stripe holds the first payout 7–14 days.
   - Shopify Payments holds it about 7–14 days, and some cases run to 21.
   - Etsy holds new-seller funds 14–20 days.
   - PayPal releases payments immediately up to an unpublished monthly limit. Above that limit it holds them up to 21 days.
   - TPT pays monthly, so it collects nothing in the window.

   So **the first paid order has to land by about 11-13**. After that, only PayPal (under its limit) and Stripe (once its first payout has gone out) can still settle sales made up to about 11-24.
2. **Paid ads to a new store fail on unit economics.**
   - Traffic: about $0.70 per click on Meta traffic campaigns (WordStream 2025), with CPMs 17–30% higher in late November.
   - Conversion: about 1.4% for an average Shopify store (Littledata, cited secondhand).
   - Together that puts the cost per sale around $50–$100 (ESTIMATE), against a $9–$35 product.
   - Paid social only works with a much higher ticket or with an audience the seller already has. Neither exists.
3. **The only line that clears the payout wall with meaningful money is (a): a fixed-price, done-for-you AI service sold to small businesses by direct outreach and paid by PayPal or Stripe invoice.** It has three things in its favour:
   - The ticket is $149–$399.
   - Claude produces the deliverable in minutes.
   - The buyer's own deadline (Black Friday and December promotions) falls inside the window.

   Its bottleneck is cold-email deliverability from a new domain, which vendors say needs 2–4+ weeks of warmup.
4. **Credible settled cash by 11-27 for this whole category is about $300–$2,000. ESTIMATE p50 ≈ $600.** None of these lines gets near $10k in the window. Run (a), with (c) using the same outreach engine, as the remote top-up beside the plan's local lines. Treat (b) as a December line that settles after 11-27.

## 3. Candidate (a) — Done-for-you AI marketing services for small businesses. **RANK 1**

**What is sold.** Fixed-price, fixed-scope packs, delivered as a Google Doc or ZIP within 48 hours:
- **Holiday Promo Pack, $199:** a 5-email Black Friday to New Year sequence, 20 social captions with an image brief, 4 Google Business Profile (GBP) posts, holiday hours copy and 1 SMS blast.
- **GBP Care, $99/month:** 4 GBP posts a month, plus replies to every new review, drafted by Claude and posted by the owner from a paste-ready doc. Or posted by us if the owner adds us as a GBP manager, which is online and free.
- **Menu or product description rewrite, $149** for up to 40 items.
- **Local landing page copy, $149** for each page in a city × service grid.

Verticals that already buy holiday promotions: restaurants and cafés, salons and spas, boutiques, florists, gyms, home-service trades, and realtors.

**Who pays.** The owner or manager, by card. $99–$199 sits under the "needs approval" threshold of a small business (ESTIMATE).

**Evidence buyers pay (PUBLISHED comparables):**
- GBP management: $125–$500 per location per month is the common range. Example tiers are $197/month for 4 posts a month and $397/month with review responses. https://merchynt.com/post/google-my-business-management-pricing ; https://kharkevich-com.pages.dev/services/small-business/google-business-profile ; https://www.humblehelp.studio/blog/google-business-profile-management-cost-bay-area
- Done-for-you email setup: AWeber's own one-time DFY service is $79, with a Black Friday DFY package at $99. https://blog.aweber.com/?p=108649 ; https://www.aweber.com/black-friday.htm
- Managed email runs $300–$2,500 a month. https://codewcg.com/resources/how-much-does-email-marketing-cost/
- Restaurant post packs: 60 days of posts for $37 (Gumroad), 50 DFY food posts for £49 (Gumroad), and holiday packs for $29.99–$39.99. These are self-serve templates, so they cap the price of a *generic* pack. The $199 premium has to come from being *specific to that business*: their menu, hours and offers. https://simplespirituality.gumroad.com/l/50restaurantposts
- No sales counts were found for done-for-you packs. **Demand at $199 is unproven: ESTIMATE.**

**Finding customers online (no bidding platforms):**
1. **Cold email.** Addresses come from business websites, Google Maps listings and state licence lists. Claude scrapes nothing paid; extraction is manual or semi-manual at 20–40 contacts an hour (ESTIMATE, as in the puzzle-lines file).
   - Send from 2–3 new mailboxes on a secondary domain at 20–30 a day each, with a personalized first line written by Claude from the business's own Google listing ("your GBP's last post was in March").
   - **Deliverability risk:** vendors say a new domain needs 2–4 weeks of warmup. Mitigations:
     - Send low volume, 10–25 a day per inbox, ramping.
     - Use plain text with no links in the first touch, and send a payment link only after a reply.
     - Use the operator's existing, aged domain or Gmail if they have one, keeping to the CAN-SPAM rules.
2. **Website contact forms and Facebook Page messages:** about 20–40 an hour, no deliverability ramp (ESTIMATE).
3. **LinkedIn connection notes** to owners of agencies and multi-location operators. Manual, free.
4. **Free sample hook:** one GBP post and three review replies written for the business, pasted *in the first email*. Claude does this in about 30 seconds. It doubles as proof of quality.

**Funnel (ESTIMATE, using the benchmarks above):**
- 1,200 first touches from 11-04 to 11-18 (3 inboxes at about 25/day for 12 days, plus about 300 forms or DMs).
- Replies: 2.5% gives 30 replies; 30–40% of replies are positive or curious (higher than the 14% vendor average because the first email already carries free value, ESTIMATE), giving about 10.
- Close: 30–40% of those, giving about 3–4 sales at $199 plus 1–2 GBP Care subscriptions at $99.

  **≈ $700–$1,000** collected. The range runs from $0 at Belkins' 0.45% to about $2,000 at top-quartile 5.5%.

**Checkout:** a PayPal invoice (sent from PayPal, the buyer pays by card without an account), or a Stripe Payment Link per pack. Ask for full prepayment; delivery follows payment.

**Remote fulfilment and automation.** A Claude prompt template per vertical, with inputs taken from the buyer's intake form or website: menu, hours, offers, tone. Output is a doc. The operator spends about 15–30 minutes per order on review (ESTIMATE). The GBP review replies are drafted by Claude, and the owner pastes them or grants manager access. No paid API is used; it runs on the Claude subscription interactively.

**Costs and margin (ESTIMATE):**
- Domain $10–$15, plus 2–3 mailboxes at about $7/month each (Google Workspace or Zoho). Under $50 at risk.
- Processor fees about 3–3.5%. Margin is about 95%.

**Uncertainties:**
- Reply rate from a cold domain.
- Willingness to pay $199 when $30–$50 template packs exist.
- Whether the PayPal monthly release limit sits below about $1,000. If it does, the excess holds up to 21 days; ask PayPal for a limit review.
- Businesses already committed their Black Friday plans in October, so the December, holiday-hours and New-Year angle carries more weight after 11-20 (ESTIMATE).

**Exact path:**
- **11-03:** Buy the secondary domain. Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Create the PayPal Business and Stripe accounts and take a $1 self-test payment on Stripe, which starts the 7–14 day first-payout clock. Write 3 vertical prompt templates and 1 sample deliverable per vertical.
- **11-03 → 11-05:** Build a 400-row list (restaurants, salons, boutiques) in 3–5 metros, one owner email per row. Start warmup at 10/day per inbox. Send contact forms and FB messages in parallel from day 1.
- **11-05 → 11-18:** Ramp to 25/day per inbox with 2 follow-ups at +3 and +7 days. Invoice within an hour of any "yes" and deliver within 48 hours.
- **11-18 → 11-24:** Pitch the "December + holiday hours + New Year" pack to every non-responder. PayPal only for these late sales.

**Kill test:**
- Fewer than 6 replies from the first 300 delivered first touches by **11-10**: abandon cold email. Keep forms and DMs only if they produced at least 1 reply.
- 0 paid orders by **11-13**: stop (a) entirely. Nothing sold after that settles except under PayPal's limit, and the line has shown no demand.

## 5. Candidate (b) — Personalized printables and POD books for consumers

### (b-Etsy) Personalized printable puzzle or activity book, $8–$15. **RANK 4 (December line)**

- **What is sold:** a PDF "{Child's name}'s Christmas Puzzle Book" or a "{Family name} Holiday Word Search" (names, pets and in-jokes as words). It is generated per order from the Etsy personalization field.
- **Evidence buyers pay:** generic Christmas activity book PDFs sell on Etsy at $2.99–$8.99. Name-personalized PDFs exist on Payhip, and in physical form on Card Factory and Not On The High Street (PUBLISHED; no sales counts found: https://payhip.com/b/Q62Kg). Free competition is heavy. Name-personalized word search is a known consumer niche (https://www.customily.com/post/personalized-word-search-puzzle-how-to-create-unique-products-that-sell).
- **Channel:** Etsy search plus Etsy Ads. Etsy Ads cost $0.10–$0.50 per click and convert 1.5–4%, so the cost per sale is about $3–$30 (ESTIMATE). A new shop with 0 reviews converts at the low end, and one guide expects 1–2 sales a week at $5/day.
- **Fulfilment:** semi-automatic. Read the name from the order, run the generator, upload or message the PDF. About 3–5 minutes per order (ESTIMATE). It is only fully automatic with the Etsy API, and the API may need app approval, so this is not counted on.
- **Settlement:** sales by about **11-06 to 11-09** for 14–20 day holds, and a new shop will not have ranking by then. **ESTIMATE $0–$200 settled by 11-27.** Sales from 11-10 to 12-20 are real, but they settle in December.
- **Costs:** $0.20 listing fee and 6.5% transaction fee (known Etsy fees, not re-verified), plus ads at $5/day. Under $100 at risk.
- **Kill test:** fewer than 2 orders by **11-16** from 20 listings and $5/day in ads: stop ads, leave the listings live.

### (b-ads) The same product on own Shopify, Payhip or Gumroad with paid Meta ads. **REJECT**

Cold traffic costs about $0.70–$1.00 per click in the Q4 CPM spike and converts at about 1–1.4%, so each sale costs **$50–$100** against a $9–$15 product (ESTIMATE from the WordStream, Superads and Littledata figures above). Shopify Payments' first-payout hold also pushes the last first-sale date to about 11-06 to 11-13. New ad accounts are capped at about $50/day. **It fails margin and timing.**

### (b-POD) Personalized physical puzzle book, Lulu Direct + Shopify (or Printful or Bookvault), $29–$35. **REJECT for the window**

- **Evidence buyers pay at this price:** Wonderbly personalized books cost from £24.99, US $45.99–$64.99. https://wetried.it/wonderbly-review/ ; https://graziadaily.co.uk/life/parenting/wonderbly-personalised-books-black-friday
- **Unit cost:** Lulu print is about $5–$8 for a black-and-white paperback, plus about $4.50 US shipping. Production takes 3–5 business days, and reviewers say checkout dates exclude it. https://scribecount.com/author-resource/publishing-wide/lulu ; https://apps.shopify.com/lulu-direct/reviews
- **Margin:** about $15–$20 a book. **Cost per sale with ads:** $50–$100. Negative.
- **Payout:** Shopify's first payout comes 7–14+ days after the first sale.
- Personalized POD also needs one Lulu project per buyer. That is manual or needs the Lulu API, which is free to call but adds integration work and print-quality risk.
- **Fails cash timing and margin.** Revisit only with an existing audience.

### Santa letters (digital). **REJECT**

$2.99–$5.25 on Etsy, Gumroad and Payhip, free alternatives everywhere (https://wolfandwonderco.gumroad.com/l/santaletter ; https://payhip.com/b/IGTme), and Etsy's 14–20 day hold on top. Volume needed is above 300 orders for $1,000. **It fails.**

## 7. Open uncertainties to verify on day 1 (each is answerable by opening the account, not by research)

- The PayPal monthly release limit amount. It is shown in the account under "Know your options".
- Whether Stripe's 7–14 days counts from a $1 test payment. Stripe says from the "first successful payment", so a live $1 charge to the operator's own card should qualify. Verify in the Dashboard.
- Whether Etsy holds digital-item funds for a new shop. Sources conflict.
- Whether the operator owns an aged domain or mailbox. If so, the deliverability risk in (a) drops materially.
