## Demand and price

- **E-2 visas issued.** FY2025: 51,047 (preliminary, State Department monthly reports, counted by IMI). FY2024: 55,324.
  - The counts include dependents, so principal applicants are fewer.
  - https://www.imidaily.com/?p=147117 · https://manifestlaw.com/blog/e2-visa-statistics-investors-should-know
- **E-2 business-plan prices.**
  - Typical range $1,500–$3,500, with $2,000–$3,000 often cited. https://www.alazlaw.com/blog/e-2-visa-cost-2026-complete-breakdown-fees-expenses/ · https://tukki.ai/blog/e2-visa-cost
  - About $1,000 when a law firm organizes the plan. https://www.beyondborderglobal.com/resources/e-2-visa-lawyer-cost-flat-fee-vs-hourly-rates-and-business-plan-support
  - $500–$2,000 per Upmetrics. https://upmetrics.co/blog/e2-visa-cost
  - Limit: these are mostly figures from law firms and vendors.
- **What a plan must contain.** Marginality, a 5-year P&L, cash flow and balance sheet, a hiring plan with titles and salaries, and source of funds.
  - Secondary, from law-firm summaries. The Foreign Affairs Manual (9 FAM) was not read.
  - https://natlawreview.com/article/e-2-visa-requirements-2026 · https://www.alazlaw.com/blog/e-2-visa-business-plan-requirements-2026/
- **Attorneys use outside plan writers.**
  - AILA hosts a page for Joorney Business Plans (E-2, L-1, E-1, EB-5).
  - Visafranchise runs an attorney partnership program.
  - Some firms write plans in-house.
  - https://www.aila.org/joorney-business-plans (search listing; page 404 when fetched) · https://www.visafranchise.com/immigration-attorney-partnerships
- **Attorney population.** AILA: 18,000+ US lawyers; the 2025 media kit says 16,000+. Business-practice digest segment: 8,000+ members.
  - https://www.aila.org/about · AILA 2025 Media Kit (PDF)
