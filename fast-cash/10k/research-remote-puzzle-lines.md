# Remote puzzle-content lines — cash collected by 2026-11-27

Research date: 2026-11-02. Window: 25 days. Constraints: location-independent, ≤$300 at risk, no paid APIs, no freelance bidding.
Asset: unlimited verified sudoku / word search / crossword-style grids, typeset to print-ready PDF at ~zero marginal cost.

Labels: **PUBLISHED** = stated on the cited page (vendor pages are vendor claims). **ESTIMATE** = my reasoning, not a published figure.
"Collected" below means money in a bank account, or withdrawable to one, by 11-27. A marketplace balance that cannot be withdrawn yet does not count.

---

## Bottom line (read this first)

1. **Payout timing decides most of this.** TPT pays November earnings in December, so it cannot collect anything in the window. A new Etsy shop's funds become depositable about 14 days after each sale, and a reserve can hold them longer, so only sales made by about 11-13 can count. A new Stripe account's first payout comes 7–14 days after its first payment. Under every option, **the first sale has to happen by about 11-13 to 11-18**.
2. **No seller with no audience is going to be found in 25 days.** Paid marketplaces (Etsy, TPT) need ranking history this seller doesn't have. The only channel that doesn't depend on being found is **direct outreach to a buyer who already pays for this kind of content**: senior-living activity directors and church children's ministries.
3. **Expected cash collected by 11-27 is realistically $0–$500 across every line (ESTIMATE).** None of these lines gets near $10k in this window. The best line is still worth running, because it costs about $0 and builds a December/January recurring base.

**Rank for cash collected by 11-27:** **A** (senior-living holiday pack, direct card/invoice) > **C-church** (digital Advent/Christmas kids puzzle bulletin) > **B** (personalized gifts: own payment link first, Etsy second) > **C-restaurant/print** > **D** (TPT, zero by construction).

## A. Senior-living large-print holiday/seasonal puzzle packs (B2B, national)

**What is sold:** A PDF "Holiday Large-Print Puzzle Pack" licensed per community to print unlimited copies in one building. Example contents: 40–60 puzzles (Thanksgiving, Christmas/Hanukkah, New Year's, winter word searches, easy sudoku, fill-ins), 18–24pt type, answer keys, plus a one-page group-activity guide. Upsell: a monthly pack at $12–19 a month, or a multi-community license for operators.

**Market size (PUBLISHED):**
- NCHS counts **32,200 residential care communities** (the category that includes assisted living), with 1,313,600 licensed beds and 988,800 residents in 2022. Source: Biennial Overview, Table 1. <https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/fastats/residential-care-communities.htm>
- The 2025 NPALS weighted estimates show about 1.0M current RCC residents. <https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/npals/2025/2025-NPALS-RCC-weighted-estimates.pdf>
- NIC puts US assisted-living stock at 717,100 units in 2020; its inventory counts only properties with 25+ units. <https://www.nic.org/blog/how-much-senior-housing-and-care-inventory-exists-in-the-united-states/>
- Nursing homes and adult day centers also employ activity staff. I didn't count them here.

**What buyers pay now (PUBLISHED comparables):**

**Price (ESTIMATE, supported by the table):** $29 for a one-time Holiday Pack facility license, or $15 a month as a "Monthly Pack" (which matches Golden Days). $29 sits between the $24.99 bundle and the $55 Etsy facility license. It's also below what a director can usually put on a card without approval, though that last point is an ESTIMATE. For an operator: $199 for up to 10 communities.

**The anchor is low.** A whole-building monthly box costs $15, and incumbents with years of content charge $200 a year. Puzzles are commodity content here. The pitch has to be "holiday-specific, large print, ready this week," not "puzzles."

**Reach:**
- State licensing lists can be downloaded but usually have **no email addresses** (PUBLISHED). Examples:
  - Illinois assisted-living xlsx (name, address, city, zip only): <https://data.illinois.gov/dataset/88864517-6d80-4463-86d6-0f2522638fd0/resource/2024ea0e-3fa2-4c67-8003-f1d42358af2f>
  - NC monthly facility files: <https://info.ncdhhs.gov/dhsr/nhlcs/facilities.html>
  - WA DSHS Excel export: <https://fortress.wa.gov/dshs/adsaapps/lookup/BHAdvLookup.aspx>
  - RI licensee download: <https://health.ri.gov/licensing/licensee-lists>
- Emails have to come by hand from each community's website or contact form, or by phone ("who runs life enrichment?"). ESTIMATE: 20–40 usable contacts per hour.
- Free: activity-director Facebook groups and NAAP or state activity associations (<https://nccap.memberclicks.net/state-and-national-associations>). Self-promotion rules vary, so offer a free sample page rather than a pitch.
- Cold B2B email is legal in the US under CAN-SPAM with an honest sender and an opt-out link. That's general knowledge; I didn't re-fetch the rule.

**Fulfilment:** A Stripe Payment Link (or PayPal) delivers the PDF by automated email, or an invoice with a pay-by-card link. There's no printing and no shipping.

**Cash needed:** $0–$20 for a domain and email, since Stripe has no monthly fee. That's well inside $300.

**Payment timing (PUBLISHED):** A new Stripe account's first payout is "typically 7-14 days after you successfully receive your first payment," then a T+2 rolling schedule. <https://docs.stripe.com/payouts>, <https://support.stripe.com/questions/common-questions-about-payout-schedules>. So the first card payment must land by **about 11-13 to 11-18** to be collected by 11-27. Net-30 invoices to corporate operators would not pay in time (ESTIMATE).

**Contribution margin (ESTIMATE):** About 87% on $29 after Stripe's ~2.9% + $0.30 (standard US card pricing, not re-fetched in this session). Content cost is ~0 and time is the real cost.

**Realistic capacity by 11-27 (ESTIMATE):**
- 600–1,000 hand-collected contacts × 1–3% purchase = **6–30 sales × $29 ≈ $175–$870 gross**. Expect the low end, because of holiday budgets and gatekeeping.
- One multi-community operator deal ($199+) would double that. Corporate procurement in 3 weeks is unlikely.

**First action (today):**
1. Build the Thanksgiving + December packs.
2. Make a free 3-puzzle sample PDF.
3. Make a Stripe Payment Link at $29.
4. Pull one state list (IL or NC), collect 100 activity-director emails from community websites, and send a plain-text email with the free sample.
5. Post the free sample in 2–3 activity-director groups.

**Main uncertainty:** Will an activity director pay $29 to a brand-new vendor when Activity Connection, Golden Carers and plenty of free printables already exist? Is a holiday/large-print angle enough to break through?

**Kill test:** After 300 contacts sent by **11-09**, stop if there's <1 purchase and <5 sample replies. Shift effort to C-church or B.

## B. Personalized puzzle gifts (custom crossword/word search with names and memories)

**What is sold:** A personalized digital crossword or word search built from the buyer's names, dates and in-jokes, delivered as a print-ready PDF within 24–48h. Price: $12–$25 (ESTIMATE). Optional upsell: "printed and framed," which means physical fulfilment, shipping and more cash at risk.

**Etsy evidence (PUBLISHED, from search snippets; Etsy returns 403 to direct fetch):**
- The category is crowded, with many listings: <https://www.etsy.com/market/custom_personalized_crossword_puzzle>
- Shop sales counts visible in results:
  - one listing's shop: **3.5k sales over 3 years**: <https://www.etsy.com/listing/1854047758/personalized-crossword-puzzle-own-text>
  - "YourCrossword": **595 sales**: <https://www.etsy.com/shop/YourCrossword>
  - a smaller custom shop: **17 sales in 1.5 years**
- Prices:
  - wedding/bridal "sip and solve" templates: ~$3–$13, larger ~$30: <https://www.etsy.com/listing/4304651948>, <https://www.etsy.com/listing/1899252419>
  - custom wedding template: $7.50
  - fully custom 15×15 printable crossword: $135: <https://www.etsy.com/listing/1857163397/crossword-puzzle-custom-gift-100>
- Free and low-cost custom-crossword sites compete for the same buyer, e.g. yourcrossword.com: <https://rachelsimon.substack.com/p/introducing-yourcrosswordcom>

**Etsy fees (PUBLISHED, via secondary sources; Etsy's fee page 403'd):**
- $0.20 listing fee
- 6.5% transaction fee
- 3% + $0.25 payment processing
- Offsite Ads 15% on attributed sales for shops under $10k/yr

Source: <https://checkoutpage.com/blog/etsy-fees>. On a $15 sale, net is about **$13.08** (ESTIMATE), or ~$10.83 if Offsite Ads attributes the sale.

**Etsy Ads:** $1/day minimum budget (PUBLISHED: <https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360033701174>). CPC is roughly $0.20–$0.60 (secondary). <https://listingview.io/blogs/how-etsy-ads-work-in-2026-and-when-to-turn-them-off>

**Etsy payout for a new seller (PUBLISHED, Etsy help):**
- "Your funds will generally be eligible for deposit **14 days after a sale**," and can take longer depending on shop standing: <https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360002080688>
- A **payment account reserve** may also apply to shops new to Etsy, holding a percentage of funds on a rolling basis (sources cite up to 45 days): <https://channelx.world/2021/01/etsy-payment-account-reserve-introduced>, <https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015747228>
- So **only Etsy sales made by about 11-13 can be collected by 11-27.**

**New-shop reality:**
- There is no rigorous published first-month data.
- Secondary or unsourced claims: ~65% of shops never sell; first sale typically takes 1–6 weeks; the median for eventually-successful shops is 8–12 weeks. <https://sellertoolshq.com/guides/etsy-success-rate/>, <https://www.insightagent.app/guides/how-much-do-you-make-on-etsy> (ESTIMATE-grade)
- Etsy forum thread on first-month sales (anecdotes): <https://community.etsy.com/t5/Lots-of-views-no-sales/How-many-sales-did-you-get-in-your-first-month/td-p/121201411>

**Alternatives:**
- **Gumroad:** 10% + $0.50 plus processing. Payouts are weekly; one source says unverified accounts need $100 before payout. <https://dodopayments.com/blogs/gumroad-fees-explained>, <https://checkoutpage.com/blog/gumroad-fees>
- **Own Shopify/Stripe link:** best payout terms (Stripe, as in A) but **no traffic**. It only works if the operator's personal network buys, e.g. posting to friends and family and local Facebook/Nextdoor. That's not "national," but it is collectible.

**Holiday dynamics:** Digital delivery avoids shipping cutoffs, and personalized gifts peak in the first half of December. Most of the demand therefore falls **after** the 11-13 Etsy deadline (ESTIMATE).

**Cash needed:** $0–$60 (listing fees for 20–40 listings, plus optional $1–2/day ads).

**Capacity by 11-27 (ESTIMATE):**
- Etsy: 0–5 sales by 11-13, so **$0–$65 collected**.
- Own payment link to the personal network: 5–20 × $15 = $75–$300, collectible only if the first Stripe payment lands by ~11-13.
- Per-order labor is real (intake, proofing, one revision), at 15–30 min each.

**First action:**
1. Open a Stripe link plus an intake form. Post to the personal network today.
2. In parallel, open an Etsy shop with 10–20 listings. This is for December, not for 11-27.

**Main uncertainty:** Etsy search visibility for a 0-review shop in a crowded, saturated category.

**Kill test:** Zero Etsy orders by **11-12** means Etsy cannot contribute to 11-27. Keep it as a December channel only and stop spending on ads.

  - Hoffmaster Color Me + crayons: $164.99 per 1,000

  - 12-page custom-cover book: $3.10/unit at 500
  - Sources: <https://www.promotionalproductinc.com/branded-spring-kids-activity-pad-one-color-imprint-included>, <https://creapub.ca/en/shop/office-workspace/stationary/coloring-books/custom-cover-activity-book-wcustom-activities-12-pages-zsjme-cyvxi>

- **Only viable form:** digital artwork only ("your logo + 2 puzzle pages, print-ready PDF, you print locally"), at $49–$99 (ESTIMATE). Expect near-zero conversions in 25 days without referrals or local relationships.

**C-church: second-best line.**
- **What is sold:** A digital Advent/Christmas kids' puzzle bulletin set (4 Advent Sundays + Christmas Eve). It includes Bible-verse word searches and a kids' sudoku/maze, with the church's name inserted. Reproducible within the congregation.
- **Comparables (PUBLISHED):**
  - Children's Worship Bulletins: $4.99–$7.49/month: <https://childrensbulletins.com/kids-bulletin>
  - Catholic Corner digital: $170/yr (school year): <https://giamusic.com/resource/catholic-corner-children-s-bulletins-digital-publication-ecb10>
  - CPH children's bulletins: 25-copy-per-quarter minimum: <https://www.cph.org/childrens-bulletins>
- **Price:** $19–$29 per set, with a church-name personalization fee included (ESTIMATE).
- **Reach:** Children's-ministry and church-office emails from church websites. Denominational directories are public, but I didn't source counts.
- **Timing:** Advent starts Sun **11-29-2026** (Christmas 12-25 is a Friday, so the fourth Sunday before it is 11-29). Churches need the files before that, so the buying window falls **inside** the collection window. That is this line's main advantage.
- **Fulfilment / cash / margin:** Same as A (Stripe link, ~87% margin, ~$0 cash).
- **Capacity (ESTIMATE):** 400–800 contacts × 1–2% = 4–16 × $25 = $100–$400.
- **Main uncertainty:** Theological and content fit, since denominations differ and incumbents bundle lessons with puzzles. Also how many church offices read cold email.
- **Kill test:** <1 sale from 250 contacts by **11-12**.

## D. Teachers Pay Teachers holiday puzzle packs: zero cash by 11-27

- **Payout (PUBLISHED, TPT help):** Monthly. Each month's earnings arrive **the following month, no later than the 21st**, usually around the 10th, via Hyperwallet. <https://help.teacherspayteachers.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042580932-How-do-I-get-paid-as-a-Seller>
  - So November sales pay out about Dec 10–21. **Cash collected by 11-27 is $0 by construction.**
- **Fees (secondary):** Basic tier has a ~$29 one-time fee, 55% payout and $0.30 per resource. Premium is $59.95/yr with 80% payout, reportedly 77.5% from 2027. <https://edubracket.com/articles/best-platforms-to-sell-teaching-resources-2026>, <https://www.tpt.it.com/fees/>
- A new store with no reviews also faces the same discovery problem as Etsy.
- **Verdict:** Kill for this goal. It's acceptable as a December/January backlog using the same generated content.
