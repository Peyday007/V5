lives or dies on plan #1.
- Scope A is not bought before proof: the cap cannot fund it alongside the
  two proof orders.

## Selection rule

Hire the first candidate who passes all six; record each answer in the ledger notes.

1. Evidenced E-2/L-1 plan experience: at least 20 plans, with redacted samples.
2. Quotes ≤ $95 per plan for the proof orders.
3. Available Dec 1–23, 24-hour turnaround, at least 2 plans a day.
4. Signs the NDA.
5. Can be paid per plan on completion. Each payment is recorded as a ledger
   SPEND, PRE before proof, POST after.
6. Passes a paid trial: a scope-A critique of the sample, after proof. Before
   proof, the first real review is the trial.

**Backup:** shortlist 3 candidates, so one cancellation does not stop
delivery.

## QA checklist (reviewer and Brain both apply it)

Every item must pass.

- Investment amount, uses of funds and source-of-funds narrative agree with
  each other and with the client's documents.
- At least 50% ownership or operational control is stated.
- Investment is shown at risk and irrevocably committed: expenditures named.
- Marginality: income capacity beyond the investor's family; a 5-year hiring
  plan with titles, salaries and start dates.
- 5-year P&L, cash flow, balance sheet and break-even. The year-1 monthly
  figures sum to the annual figures. Cash never goes negative without
  financing shown.
- Every market statistic has a source and date. No invented figure; the
  investor's assumptions are labelled as such.
- No legal conclusions stated as ours; the attorney is named as deciding use.
- Formatting: the attorney's requested structure, page numbers, exhibit
  references.
