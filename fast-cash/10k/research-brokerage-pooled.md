# Brokerage, pooled-demand and subcontracting options — cash collected by 2026-11-27

> **Corrected 2026-11-12 (§105):** government and federal work is not excluded. Every opening is judged on its economics, its time to revenue and feasibility, never its industry. A long award cycle may not suit a 30-day sprint, and it stays eligible for longer goals.

Researched 2026-11-02 with web search. **PUBLISHED** means the figure appears at the
cited URL. **ESTIMATE** means my own arithmetic or judgment, built from the published
figures it names. Many cost-guide pages in this niche are SEO content farms. Those are
not cited, and the figures below come from operators, suppliers, Angi/HomeAdvisor
numbers quoted by Jobber, or trade associations.

Constraints applied: one person plus 2 part-time helpers; cash COLLECTED by Nov 27
(25 days); ≤$300 at risk before income; ≤$3,000 committed; must work in most US metros;
no government work, domain speculation, freelance-platform bidding or paid APIs.

## 1. Holiday-light installation brokerage (+ gutter clean on the same ladder visit)

- **What is sold:** a booked, fixed-price roofline/shrub light install (lights included,
  or installed using the customer's own), with a January takedown included or sold as
  an add-on. A gutter clean done on the same visit is an upsell.
- **Buyers:** owners of single-family homes in middle- to upper-income subdivisions.
  Older owners and two-story homes, who want to stay off ladders, are the best fit.
- **Reach channel:** two helpers door-hanging or knocking in 2–3 subdivisions each
  weekend, posts in Nextdoor and local Facebook groups (organic, no paid ads), and a
  yard sign on each finished house.
- **Price and evidence:**
  - PUBLISHED: Angi 2025 average of $442 for a U.S. holiday-light install. Roof
    lighting runs $150–$2,000 and a full package $750–$5,000. Removal is $100–$400.
    Jobber quotes these figures:
    https://getjobber.com/academy/holiday-lighting/how-to-bid-christmas-light-installation/
  - PUBLISHED: Homeyou gives $329–$882 for 2026, with an average of $564:
    https://www.homeyou.com/christmas-light-installation-cost
  - PUBLISHED margin guidance: Jobber's example uses a 30% margin
    ($2,000 ÷ 0.7 = $2,857). One operator quoted there says his lighting division
    "runs close to 40% net profit". Same Jobber URL.
- **Fulfilment / subcontract:**
  - PUBLISHED subcontractor pay: a Denver lighting company pays independent
    subcontractors $150–$250 per completed day. Installer pay is $15–$30/hr generally.
    Both appear in the search summary of ZipRecruiter and Career.com postings:
    https://www.career.com/job/colorado-light-group/christmas-light-installer/j202510010308377770488
  - PUBLISHED fallback if no crew will take subcontract terms: lighting companies pay
    outside reps 8% of gross revenue on company leads and 10% on self-generated leads
    (Astoria Lighting Co.):
    https://astorialightingco.com/blog/career/outside-sales-representative/
    Humbug Holiday Lighting pays $15/hr plus 30% of gross profit:
    https://ph-hhl-careers.careerplug.com/jobs/3552000
    A floor therefore exists: even as a pure referral rep the operator earns 8–10% of
    revenue from established companies.
  - Partner crews: existing gutter, window-washing, pressure-washing and landscaping
    crews, who are already insured and whose season ends in November. ESTIMATE:
    subcontract at 60–70% of the customer price, with the operator keeping 30–40%.
- **Cash needed:** ESTIMATE ~$150–$300 for door hangers, 10 yard signs and a
  free-tier booking/payment page. No inventory: lights are bought per job only after
  the customer has paid. Have your own general-liability certificate, or require the
  crew's certificate naming you, before the first install.
- **Payment timing:** take a 50% deposit at booking and the balance on install day.
  Card payments via Square or Stripe settle in 1–2 business days. Install dates run
  Nov 8–26, before Thanksgiving, which is when demand peaks. Everything is collected
  before Nov 27.
- **Realistic deals in 25 days:** ESTIMATE 12–25 installs at a $400–$550 average,
  giving $5,000–$12,000 collected. A 2-person crew does 2–4 homes a day.
- **Contribution:** ESTIMATE 25–30% after crew and lights, so $1,300–$3,500. On the
  referral-only fallback it is 8–10%, so $400–$1,200.
- **First action (today):** call 10 gutter, window-washing and landscaping companies
  in the chosen metro and ask: "Will you install lights I book, at $X per linear foot,
  insured, this month?" At the same time, put 200 door hangers out in one subdivision
  with a dated "book by Nov 15" price.
- **Main uncertainty:** crew capacity in peak weeks. Established lighting firms are
  full by mid-November. The second uncertainty is that some states (for example NJ, MD
  and CT) require home-improvement contractor registration for the party contracting
  with the homeowner. Check the target state before booking.
- **Kill test (by Nov 9):** fewer than 3 paid deposits from roughly 400 door contacts,
  OR no insured crew that accepts ≤70% of the price → drop to the 8–10% referral
  model with a local lighting company, or stop.

## 2. Pre-paid Christmas-tree delivery & setup (with a local lot or farm)

- **What is sold:** "Reserve now, delivered and set up in your stand on a date you
  choose, Nov 28 – Dec 7." The tree, delivery and setup are sold as one bundle; stand
  and disposal pickup are add-ons.
- **Buyers:** the same subdivisions as option 1, apartments and condos without trucks,
  and offices.
- **Price and evidence:**
  - PUBLISHED: a pre-cut NC Fraser fir averaged about $100 on a retail lot in 2025:
    https://m.washingtontimes.com/news/2025/dec/11/trump-tariffs-drive-artificial-christmas-tree-prices-real-saplings/
  - PUBLISHED: Cornell Farms sells "Marriage Saver Tree Setup" at $149:
    https://cornellfarms.com/products/marriage-saver-tree-setup
    Benken's sells a $300 "Kris Kringle Package" (tree delivery + setup + extras):
    https://shop.benkens.com/item/638708983105/Fraser-Fir-Tree-6-7ft?cid=43
  - PUBLISHED: 84% of growers did not plan to raise wholesale prices in 2025
    (Real Christmas Tree Board survey, same Washington Times URL).
  - I did not find a published 2026 wholesale price for a 6–7 ft Fraser fir. Ask the lot.
- **Fulfilment:** a tree lot or choose-and-cut farm supplies tagged trees; the helpers
  deliver with a pickup truck or rented van. ESTIMATE: the lot gives a 10–20%
  volume discount or a fixed referral, and the operator keeps the delivery/setup fee
  of $60–$100 per tree.
- **Cash needed:** ESTIMATE ~$100–$200 for flyers and a reservation page. No tree is
  bought until it has been paid for.
- **Payment timing:** full prepayment at reservation (Nov 3–27), so cash is collected
  before Nov 27. The service is performed after Nov 27, which leaves a refund
  liability until delivery.
- **Realistic deals:** ESTIMATE 25–60 reservations at $150–$200 bundle price, giving
  $3,000–$8,000 collected. Tree cost to the lot comes out of that, and the operator
  keeps roughly $50–$100 per tree.
- **First action:** visit 3 lots or farms this week and agree a per-tree price for
  reserved trees, plus pickup windows. Add "reserve your tree" to every option-1 door
  hanger.
- **Main uncertainty:** whether a lot will grant a discount or exclusivity. If it
  will not, the margin is the delivery fee only. Also, lots open around Thanksgiving,
  so delivery capacity on Nov 28 – Dec 1 is the constraint.
- **Kill test (by Nov 15):** fewer than 10 prepaid reservations → stop marketing and
  refund.

## 3. Small-business holiday gift boxes (prepaid; the pooled bulk-buy idea, reframed)

- **The pooled-price-break thesis largely FAILS for branded goods.** Quantity breaks
  and setup charges apply per design or imprint, so ten businesses with ten logos are
  ten small orders.
  - PUBLISHED: 4imprint's 16 oz steel tumbler costs $8.79 at 50, $7.75 at 250 and
    $6.69 at 1,000. The listing is on 4imprint.ca and is probably in CAD:
    https://www.4imprint.ca/product/C7397-C/Basic-Colour-Steel-Tumbler-16-oz
    Setup charges are $45–$65 per order:
    https://www.4imprint.com/product/157121-FC/Refresh-Vacuum-Wine-Tumbler-10-oz-Full-Color
    Pooling 5 businesses × 50 into one 250-unit run saves about 12%, and only if
    everyone accepts one imprint.
  - Holiday cards: Vistaprint shows under $1 per card at 100+ (US). The price is per
    design, so pooling does not help.
    https://www.vistaprint.com/holiday/christmas-cards/templates/kwanzaa-greeting
  - Pooling does work for **unbranded** goods (one box design, many buyers) or for
    one co-branded box, for example a business-association or chamber gift.
- **What is sold instead:** curated holiday gift boxes ($40–$75) of local or wholesale
  goods, with an optional sticker or card bearing the buyer's logo, which avoids
  imprint setup. Sold to realtors, mortgage brokers, insurance agents, dental and
  medical offices and contractors for their clients and staff.
- **Price and evidence:**
  - PUBLISHED: an average employee gift of $62 and a median of $25 in 2025
    (Tremendous platform data):
    https://www.tremendous.com/blog/employee-holiday-gifting-trends/
    An earlier ASI survey found $79:
    https://asicentral.com/press-releases/asi-survey-says-companies-spending-an-average-79-on-employee-holiday-gifts/
  - PUBLISHED: the average promotional-products distributor margin was 37.8% in 2024
    (ASI Counselor), and PPAI 100 gross margin is about 36%:
    https://www.ppai.org/media-hub/2024-ppai-100-distributor-benchmarking-business-practices-of-industry-leaders/
- **Fulfilment:** wholesale goods from Faire or local makers. PUBLISHED: Faire's
  Net-60 terms are subject to a credit assessment, and an ineligible retailer pays at
  shipment:
  https://www.faire.com/support/articles/360059487952
  Assume no terms. Assembly is done by the helpers.
- **Cash needed:** ~$150–$300 for 2–3 sample boxes. Everything else is bought only
  after a 100% prepaid order.
- **Payment timing:** 100% upfront at order, with delivery Dec 8–19. Cash is
  collected before Nov 27 if orders close by then.
- **Realistic deals:** ESTIMATE 3–8 orders × 15–30 boxes × $50, giving
  $1,500–$6,000 collected and $500–$2,000 contribution at about 35%.
- **First action:** build one $50 sample box and walk it into 20 realtor, insurance
  and dental offices. Pitch "order by Nov 21, delivered by Dec 15".
- **Main uncertainty:** many businesses have already ordered from Snappy or Vistaprint,
  and sales cycles in an office take more than a week.
- **Kill test (by Nov 14):** no prepaid order from the first 40 in-person pitches →
  stop.

## 4. Holiday wreath / poinsettia fundraiser brokerage

- **What rep commission actually exists (Q1):**
  - Otis Spunkmeyer (FAQ dated 2026-02) sells only through "local fundraising
    distributor partners", with "a sales representative who serves as a liaison".
    Boxes sell for $20–$25. Profit percentages "vary" and are not published:
    https://www.otisspunkmeyer.com/sites/default/files/2026-02/OS-FunRaising-FAQs-Proof5.pdf
    Rep commission is not published. The route is to join a distributor as a commission
    rep, but cookie-dough delivery takes 2–3 weeks and rep pay follows delivery, so it
    misses Nov 27.
  - Mrs. Fields: the organisation keeps 40%, and the guidance is to allow 2 weeks to
    sell and 2 weeks for delivery (search summary of mrsfields.com fundraising blog
    pages). There is no rep program on its pages.
  - Little Caesars: $6 per kit to the group, with delivery minimums around 50 kits.
    The program runs direct with no broker layer:
    https://www.pizzakit.ca/fundraising-profit
  - Butter Braid: $6+ per item to the group, sold through 75+ exclusive-territory
    dealers:
    https://butterbraid.com/fundraising/
    A Country Maid dealership needs about $10k–$15k invested
    (https://franchisegator.com/dealerships/country-maid), which exceeds the cap.
  - Racine Danish Kringles: "40% or more" to organisations, sold direct:
    https://www.kringle.com/fundraising
  - Equal Exchange: close to 50% profit, full cases, no minimum, direct:
    https://www.equalexchange.coop/fundraiser
  - Lynch Creek Farm wreaths: the group earns 10% of sales, there is no minimum,
    drop-ship runs to Dec 13, and there is no rep or affiliate layer:
    https://lynchcreekwreaths.com/pages/fundraising
    Sports Wreath Shop affiliate: 10%, 7-day cookie, $100 PayPal minimum:
    https://www.sportswreathshop.com/sports-wreath-shop-affiliate-program/
    Payout timing is unlikely to land before Nov 27.
  - **Conclusion:** no supplier found publishes an independent-rep commission. The
    consumer brands sell direct to the organisation, or through territory
    distributors. The only workable broker model is **buy wholesale from a nursery,
    sell to the organisation at a fixed price, and keep the spread.**
- **Spread evidence (PUBLISHED):**
  - Armstrong Garden Centers 2026: 6" poinsettia costs the group $14.30 (25–49) down
    to $13.00 (500+) against a $17 minimum sell price. A 24" wreath costs $30 against
    a $45 minimum sell. The order deadline was Fri Oct 30, which has PASSED for 2026.
    https://www.armstronggarden.com/fundraiser
  - McCarthy Family Farm (2018): wreath wholesale $18.50, delivered free on 50+ within
    100 miles, suggested retail $28–$36, bulk deliveries to Dec 15:
    https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0071/2956/3191/files/Wreath-Fundraiser-Letter-2018-1.pdf
  - Group profit benchmarks: about $10 per wreath at $30–$38 retail
    (docs.google.com sheet and Wiegand's 2022 PDF:
    https://www.wiegandsnursery.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/CLICK-HERE.pdf).
- **Model (ESTIMATE):** source wreaths at $18–$25 from a regional grower or wholesale
  florist, sell to the organisation at $25–$30, and the organisation sells at $35–$40.
  The broker keeps $4–$7 per wreath and handles the online order page, tally, delivery
  and sort.
- **Timing:** sign the organisation by Nov 9, sell Nov 9–19, the organisation pays the
  broker at order submission (about Nov 20), and delivery runs Nov 28 – Dec 5. Cash
  from the organisation lands before Nov 27. Most school organisations committed to
  fall sales in September and October (deadlines seen: Oct 29, Nov 1, Nov 4, 2025), so
  the realistic buyers are churches, scouts, sports clubs and HOAs that have not yet
  run a holiday sale.
- **Realistic deals:** ESTIMATE 2–5 organisations × 60–150 wreaths, giving $1,000–$4,000
  collected from organisations and $300–$1,200 contribution.
- **First action:** get a written 2026 wholesale quote, with a delivery date, from 2
  regional wreath growers or wholesale florists. Then call 15 church, scout and club
  treasurers.
- **Main uncertainty:** grower capacity and willingness to quote late, and whether the
  organisation will add a middleman when it could call the nursery directly.
- **Kill test (by Nov 8):** no grower quote ≤$25 delivered, OR zero organisations signed
  → stop.

## 6. Seasonal snow-contract brokerage — conditional (snow metros only)

- PUBLISHED: seasonal residential contracts cost $300–$700 and a visit $51–$203
  (Angi, April 2026, via Housecall Pro):
  https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/snow-removal-prices/
- Prepaid in November, so cash is collected. **Fails "most US metros"**, and the
  operator owns a service obligation through March. If a subcontractor no-shows after a
  storm, the refund and liability sit with the operator. Plow routes are usually
  fixed by October. Pursue it only if the operator settles in a snow metro, and only
  for already-insured plow operators who have open slots.

| Rep for cookie-dough, pizza-kit or catalog fundraisers (Otis, Mrs. Fields, Little Caesars, QSP-type) | No published independent-rep commission. Brands sell direct or through territory distributors. Delivery takes 2–3+ weeks after the sale closes, so rep pay lands after Nov 27. |

| Buying Thumbtack/Angi leads | Counts as platform bidding (excluded). PUBLISHED estimates are $25–$75 per Thumbtack lead and $15–$85 per Angi lead plus about $300/yr, from third-party blogs only. These serve solely as a cost comparison: door canvassing is cheaper. |

## Cross-cutting uncertainties

- Home-improvement contractor registration and consumer-contract rules vary by state
  (for example NJ, MD, CT, NY counties, FL). Verify them for whichever metro is chosen
  before taking deposits for lights or snow work.
- Insurance: require each subcontractor to name the operator as additional insured on
  its GL policy (ESTIMATE: no cost to the operator). Without that, carry your own GL
  before any rooftop work.
- Prepaid service that is delivered after Nov 27 (trees, gift boxes, snow) is
  collected cash carrying a refund liability, not earned revenue.
