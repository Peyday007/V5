# $10k collected revenue plan, Nov 2 to Nov 27, 2026 (central PA)

Status: research and plan. Nothing has been sold or validated yet. Every conversion number below is an **ASSUMPTION** unless it is marked **PUBLISHED** with a URL. "Revenue" means gross cash collected from customers, before costs. Pass-through cost (trees, wreaths, gift contents) is counted inside revenue and shown separately as **contribution** (revenue minus direct cost). PA sales tax collected is not revenue and is left out.

Location assumption: Mechanicsburg / Hampden Twp / Camp Hill / Harrisburg West Shore. If Peyton is somewhere else, the local supplier and competitor names change but the arithmetic still applies.

## 1. Arrangements evaluated

### A. Fall cleanup: leaf cleanup + single-story gutter clean (team does the work) — **KEEP**
- **Sold:** one visit to rake or blow leaves to the curb or bag them, plus single-story gutter clearing. Two-story gutters are declined.
- **Buyers:** homeowners in Hampden Twp, Camp Hill, Mechanicsburg Borough and Lower Allen. Older homeowners and busy families are the main targets. Leaf fall ends around mid/late November, so **this window closes about Nov 22**.
- **Price evidence (PUBLISHED):**
  - Harrisburg gutter cleaning: Angi average $144, typical $112–173 (https://match.angi.com/tloc/Harrisburg-PA/Gutters-Downspouts-Clean).
  - HomeBlue: $70–180 (https://www.homeblue.com/gutter-cleaning/harrisburg-pa-gutter-cleaning-cost.htm).
  - GreenPal Harrisburg leaf removal: average booked $46 (https://www.yourgreenpal.com/leaf-removal/harrisburg-pa).
  - HomeBlue leaf removal: $40–310 by yard size (https://www.homeblue.com/leaf-removal/harrisburg-pa-leaf-removal-cost.htm).
- **Our price (ESTIMATE):** gutters $129 alone, leaves $79–149, bundle **$165 average**. Paid on completion by Square or cash. PA sales tax applies to the leaf part.
- **Fulfilment:** rakes, tarps, a leaf blower, an extension-pole gutter scoop and a step ladder. Budget up to $150 if nothing is owned (ESTIMATE). Leaves go to the municipal curbside collection, which must be confirmed per municipality before selling. A 2026 Mechanicsburg/Hampden schedule was not found online; call Hampden Twp on 717-909-7145.

- **Margin (ESTIMATE):** about 85% contribution, since the team's own labour is not a cash cost.

- **First action:** Peyton calls Hampden Twp and Mechanicsburg Borough to confirm curbside leaf dates. Then print the combined door-hanger card (all three home services) and set up Square.
- **Main uncertainty:** whether leaves are already handled by the time the card lands. Gutters also carry fall and liability risk.

### Rejected (and why)

The economics only pay a referral fee: junk leads are worth about $25–65 to haulers, and average jobs are about $241 (https://www.webtonic.io/industry/junk-removal ; https://pushleads.com/junk-removal-marketing/junk-removal-cost-per-lead-2/). November is low moving season.
- **Snow-removal seasonal contracts brokered to plow operators.**
  - Harrisburg per-push $40–60 (https://www.homeblue.com/snow-removal/harrisburg-pa-snow-removal-cost.htm).
  - No published seasonal price was found, and no operator with spare capacity is known.
  - We would carry the no-show liability all winter, and per-push cash mostly falls after Nov 27.
  - **Optional referral-only test** if a plow operator offers a fee.
- **Estate/consignment resale.** Commission is real (35–50% in PA, https://www.homelight.com/blog/estate-sale-companies-erie-pa/). But time-to-sell is slow and only the commission is honest revenue (FB local pickup is fee-free; https://www.nifty.ai/post/facebook-marketplace-fee). This is a reserve option for idle friend-hours only.
- **Black Friday retail arbitrage.** Needs capital and carries inventory risk. eBay takes about 13.6% + $0.40 (https://linkmybooks.com/blog/what-percentage-does-ebay-take-from-sales-in-2026-full-fee-breakdown). Sales land on or after Nov 27, with the payout after the window.
- **MTG precons / deckbuilding service.** Precons sell at MSRP $29.99–49.99 with no reseller margin (https://games.gg/news/mtg-commander-precon-decks-for-2026/). A deck-advice market at local game stores is too small to move $10k, and the events belong to the store.
- **Holiday light subcontracting for a cut only.** Local installers are booked solid. Two-story/roof work without insurance is unacceptable risk.
- **Tickets/events, tutoring matchmaking.** Capital or speculative (tickets). Slow, low-ticket, Thanksgiving-interrupted (tutoring).

### What reaching the targets requires
- **$9,000 in BASE:** the non-door-hanger lines in base (warm 15 orders ≈ $3,185, wreaths $1,350, B2B $2,200, puzzles $810) give about $7,545. Each door-hanger response is worth about $103 (50% close × about $206 average order). So **$9k needs only about 14 hanger responses = 0.5% of 3,000**, *if* the warm-network and B2B assumptions hold. Base is not fragile to the hanger rate. It **is** fragile to warm (15 buyers) and B2B (2 orders). Losing both B2B orders and half the warm buyers drops base to about $9.9k, still just above $9k. Also halving the hanger rate to 1% drops it to about $6.8k.
- **$8,000 in ADVERSE:** **not supported by the evidence.** The honest gap is about **$3,900**. Closing it would need any one of these:
