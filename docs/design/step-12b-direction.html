Four things I need your judgment on

Everything else in this direction follows from the reconciled contract. These four do not — each is a real fork where your preference decides, and I have marked which way I would go.

01

Where Build and Connected sites live

§5 asks for a tight six — Russell, Work, Projects, Knowledge, Fleet, Needs you — but Build (the software factory) and Connected sites are working capabilities that must stay easy to find. Today's rail has all eight flat, which is part of why it reads cluttered.

A
Six primary, two below a rule

What the mocks show. Six get icons and weight; Build and Connected sites sit under a hairline as plain text, always visible on desktop, reachable from the More sheet on phone. Deep links keep working.

B
Build and Connected sites become project subviews

They move inside a project, beside Map / Work / Knowledge. Tighter rail, but Build is not really per-project and would feel misfiled.

C
Keep all eight equal

No hierarchy to learn. But it is the current layout, and the flatness is part of what you rejected.

02

What replaces “0 of 8 settled”

The old line was true and useless: it read as “nothing accomplished” over a project holding 57 accepted findings and a filed report. A project needs a maturity word and a shape, and the question is how much shape.

A
Maturity word + per-foundation strip

What the mocks show: Forming, then five foundations with partial bars and the rest summarised as “+3 not started”. Honest about partial work, no invented percentage.

B
Maturity word only

Quieter. But it hides that one foundation is nearly done and another is untouched, which is exactly the difference you would act on.

C
Count of answered questions instead of foundations

“73 of 83 counties.” Sharpest when the project has one dominant question; falls apart when it has four.

03

How present Russell is

§6 asks for a Brain Pulse that makes Russell feel active without becoming status spam. The line in the mocks reads “Working — auditing the county follow-up it started for itself” and breathes once every few seconds. That is a deliberate small amount of personality; it could be more or none.

A
One live line, always, in Russell's voice

Present on home only, derived from real rows, never a spinner. Says what it is thinking about, not what state machine it is in.

B
The same line on every screen

Stronger presence. Risks becoming furniture people stop reading, which §6 warns against.

C
Status only, no voice

“1 of 1 worker busy · checked 14s ago.” Calmer and colder. Loses the thing that makes it feel like a colleague.

04

Whether the constellation is the project's front door

§20 makes the constellation the primary project experience. In the mocks it sits at the top of the project page above the written summary. That is a strong claim: it means you see shape before you read words, every time you open a project.

A
Map first, summary under it

What the mocks show. The shape of the thing is the fastest read once a project has more than a handful of ideas, and it is where §20 points.

B
Summary first, map one tab along

Words before shape. Better on a phone and for a project with three ideas; weaker as the graph grows.

C
Map first on desktop, summary first on phone

Honest to each device, but the two then disagree about what a project is, and §21 says desktop and mobile share one architecture.
