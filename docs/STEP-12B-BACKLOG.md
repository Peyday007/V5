# Step 12B backlog, and what is deliberately after v1

Two sections, and the boundary between them is load-bearing. **Step 12B
completes Brain v1.** It is not the final form of the Brain, and the Post-v1
section exists so that ambitious work has somewhere to be recorded that is not
12B.

Nothing here is built in Step 12A. Anything that turns out to break the A loop
— conversation → judgment → ranked mission → the existing fleet and quality
pipeline → grounded result → knowledge update → next mission — is not a backlog
item; it is an A defect, and it goes back into `docs/STEP-12A-PLAN.md`.

A Step 12B build prompt requires the dedicated visual, mobile and interaction
review first. The Step 12A wireframe waiver does not carry forward.

---

## Step 12B — complete Brain v1

### Interface

- The finished Russell, Work, Projects, Knowledge, Fleet and Needs You
  experiences, rather than thin projections over one canonical API.
- Full visual refinement, hierarchy, density and interaction design.
- The entire approved **mobile-first rebuild**: progressive disclosure, no
  three-column compression, conversation and Needs You immediately reachable,
  maps with usable pan/zoom/focus, one shared hierarchy across desktop and
  phone.
- The entire approved **interactive 2D mapping system**: system map, workflow,
  knowledge map, decision tree, timeline, money flow — clickable components with
  their purpose, connections, state, supporting knowledge, conversations and
  active work; move, group, hide detail; "show me what breaks if this is
  removed".

### Conversations

- Collections and category organisation.
- Major unfinished conversations pinned; ordinary unfinished ones marked.
- Active/recent and finished sections.
- Tailored conversation starters derived from the real idea and its unresolved
  work.
- Advanced search, cross-conversation linking, saved views.
- Ordering by meaning, importance and unfinished state rather than recency.

### Judgment and discovery

- **Discovery Frontier v1**: the living frontier map — solid ground, weak
  ground, open questions, unexamined areas, new paths — and the lenses that scan
  it. Bounded but real. Step 12A stores the candidates, gaps, assumptions and
  contradictions it needs; this is what reads them.
- Richer ranking controls, comparisons and decision histories.
- More autonomous exploration and cross-project transfer.

### Work, Projects, Knowledge

- The complete Work interface: controls, filters, histories, "move this up",
  "why is this above Travel Booking?", "give this everything available".
- The complete living-project experience and the collaboration foundation.
- The complete v1 Knowledge experience and richer connections — still not the
  final knowledge organ.

### Fleet and Capability Lab

- The full Fleet control centre: manual scale, policy-based scaling within
  authorized bounds, temporary boost expiring to a named base, safe automatic
  resumption after a provider window resets, add/remove/cool/quarantine without
  code changes, and a direct explanation of what currently prevents further
  scale.
- The **Capability Lab**: one-Routine fit across workload shapes; the
  work-layout tournament with rotated order and repeats; quality under pressure;
  fleet, provider and recovery tests; the result contract labelling every number
  `MEASURED` / `INFERRED` / `UNKNOWN` / `PROVIDER_ENFORCED`; idea economics and
  backlog forecast; apply-as-canary, promote, retest and roll back.

### People and collaboration

- Deeper collaboration: normal-interface invitations, access management, shared
  project conversations, attribution and conflict handling.
- **A versioned per-account preference foundation** — empty, installed only if
  it fits cleanly. This item must not disappear because it is deferred: it is
  the seam the post-v1 personalization work needs to exist, and it is not the
  survey.

### Reporting and presence

- Richer personality, notifications, **Brain Pulse** (what Russell is currently
  thinking about) and the subtle **Why this matters** motivational space
  grounded in real progress. Not badges, streaks or gamification.

### Sites

- The standardized connection contract across multiple sites: state, events,
  metrics, knowledge, actions and permission boundaries; broader cross-site
  reasoning and authorized actions.

### Numbers

- Presentation refinement only. Advanced mathematical architecture is post-v1.

### Legacy

- Move genuinely missing controls into their proper new locations, complete
  parity, redirect ordinary legacy routes, retain only useful diagnostics in
  Technical/Admin, and delete obsolete legacy rendering once nothing depends on
  it. A permanent old-dashboard/new-dashboard split is not an acceptable
  end state.

## Post-v1 — deeper refinement and new organs

Recorded here so that nothing below is smuggled into 12B because it is
exciting.

- **The full per-account customization survey and behavioural
  personalization**: onboarding test, communication style, detail level,
  homepage and visual preferences, notification tolerance, interest and priority
  emphasis, behavioural learning, deeply custom layouts and personality. This is
  not v1 and is not 12B. The 12B item above is the *foundation*, not the survey.
- Dedicated research into scientific discovery, abstraction, analogy, hypothesis
  generation, causal reasoning, novelty search and unknown-unknown discovery **at
  maximum strength**.
- Advanced mathematical, statistical, optimization, forecasting, simulation,
  engineering, financial and quantitative organs — Brain conducts, specialized
  engines calculate, connected knowledge defines the variables, verification
  checks the result.
- A 3D component builder and live simulation environment.
- Much richer personality and a deeply developed motivational space.
- Continuous social-media intelligence and other new ingestion organs.
- A standardized autonomous software factory beyond what the existing research
  pipeline needs.
- Local hardware organs — local models, private compute, browser, media,
  simulation. Explicitly **not** a Step 12 dependency: the Brain is cloud
  operated and works while the laptop is closed.

## Moved out of Step 12B by the v3 addendum — 2026-09-04

Four items that were listed here are now Step 12A completion conditions,
because a Brain that runs and cannot show what it holds has not finished
Brain Ignition. They are struck from this backlog rather than deleted, so a
reader can see the boundary moved and when.

- **A usable data-backed read layer** — Work, Ideas, Knows, Who and Needs You
  projecting real authorized state, with honest empty, stale, unavailable and
  forbidden distinctions. → Step 12A, §10.1 of the plan.
- **Visible project, site and major-idea progress** — milestone-backed, never
  invented. → Step 12A, §10.2.
- **A basic read-only living constellation** — real data, site → major idea →
  ordinary idea, desktop and phone. → Step 12A, §10.3.
- **Provider-independent streamed conversation**, with model comparison,
  configurable routing, a retrieval-based context hat and asynchronous strong
  review. → Step 12A, §10.4.

**What Step 12B still owns from those areas**, and is not started here: richer
animation and visual identity; user-arranged layouts; broad cross-site
constellations; multiple specialised maps; simulation, alternative-comparison
and "what breaks if removed"; advanced search, filtering and collaboration;
polished conversation collections and tailored starters; richer fleet
controls; preference foundations; and final mobile-first refinement.

**"Step 12B refinement" is not available as a way to defer a missing Step 12A
usability requirement again.** That is the addendum's rule and it is recorded
here, in the backlog, because this file is where such a deferral would be
written.
