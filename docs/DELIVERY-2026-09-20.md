## 1. The usable work register

**Where:** the **Work** destination, leading the page.
**Over HTTP:** `GET /api/register`, plus the workstream routes.
**Documented:** `docs/WORK-REGISTER.md`. Rules: CLAUDE.md §39, invariant 42.

It answers the six questions — *what is pursuing money, what is being built,
what is running, what is blocked, what needs me, what actually shipped* — from
rows, on every read, with the row that decided each answer printed beside it.

Each workstream shows its intended outcome, its sources (conversations and
passages), its related campaigns, branches and pull requests, its derived state
with the evidence for it, its blockers, its next action, and whether a person is
needed. One conversation can feed several workstreams and several can feed one.

## 2. The conversation entrance

**Credential:** `brnc_…`, minted in a browser at `POST /api/bridge/credentials`,
shown once and recoverable afterwards by nobody.
**Documented:** `docs/CONVERSATION-BRIDGE.md`, including the OpenAPI document to
paste into a ChatGPT custom action and the instruction that makes it call on
every turn.

**Identity.** It resolves to the **person** and to nothing else — there is no
column on `bridge_credentials` that could name a worker. It deliberately drops
Brain administration, so it reaches the projects that person holds a membership
row on and not the ones they administer. It cannot mint another credential. A
worker is refused by type at every route, reads included.

**When synchronization happens.** When the client calls the action. Nothing
pushes from ChatGPT and nothing here polls it. A missed sync is visible as
`lastSyncAt`, `messageCount`, and the positions a receipt names as never given.
**The manual import is a fallback and is not the automatic bridge working.**

## 6. Two defects, both found by running rather than reading

**A blocked campaign resumed in the wrong stage.** `NO_HEALTHY_EXECUTION_SURFACE`
is raised from two places and `unblockStage` resumed both into `EXECUTING`; a
campaign with nothing planned then stopped at *"Nothing was integrated, so there
is no change to review"* — true, and the wrong diagnosis. Fixed by deriving the
resume target from the rows.

**Authorizing too early made a change unauthorizable for ever.** Pressing
Authorize once without acceptance conditions created a contract with none, and
every later attempt collided with that row and was refused in the same words —
the screen named a remedy and applying it did nothing. Fixed through the
amendment ledger, on an unapproved contract that has no conditions, by the
person authorizing.

Both regression tests were run against a neutered fix to watch them fail before
they were trusted to pass.
