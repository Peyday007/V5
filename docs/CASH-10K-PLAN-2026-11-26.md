## 0. Verdict

**$10,000 in 30 days is not supported at high confidence by any plan the evidence allows.**

- **The strongest plan** (§3) reaches $10,000 gross in about **17%** of modelled
  worlds.
  - p10 / p50 / p90 gross: **$0 / $2.9k / $13.6k**.
  - About **42%** of worlds reach $5k or more.
  - These are coverage figures over stated ranges, not calibrated
    probabilities. No resolved outcomes exist to calibrate against.
- **Brain agrees.** Its own acceptance gate reads `NEEDS_EVIDENCE` /
  `CANDIDATE_ONLY` (goal-plan read 2026-11-26T05:40Z). Its committee says the
  proven facts alone support $0, and three earlier rebuilds (11-02, 11-09,
  11-10) reached the same verdict.
- **Why run it anyway:**
  - it is the only route with a *measured* buyer signal;
  - its money-at-risk is capped at about $400–$1,500;
  - its first 7 days produce the numbers that are still missing (close rate, phone interest rate). Those numbers decide whether to keep going.

## 3. Economics (model `fast-cash/10k/plan-model-2026-11-26.py`)

The model runs 20,000 worlds. One shared "December / unknown-seller" factor
moves every rate in a world up or down together, so the two lines rise and fall
as one, as they would in practice.

| Input | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| A: email interest per lead | Beta(6, 449), mean 1.3% | **Measured** (5 of 453) |
| A: the 5 stale warm leads still convertible | 35% each | ASSUMPTION |
| A: phone reach of a decision maker × interest | 15–35% × 3–12%, over ~900 dials | ASSUMPTION. Unmeasured; measured from day 3 |
| Interested → paid | 15–45% | ASSUMPTION. No close has ever been recorded |
| B: interest per lead | Beta(1, 386), mean 0.26% | **Measured analog** (AI Dispatch) |
| B: leads reached | 1,800–3,000 | Brain's share of about 930 a day over about 15 sending days |
| B: price / cost per 5,000-home drop | $2,600–2,900 / $2,150–2,400 | Comparables: Oppizi about $2,150 all-in; MPA $2,325–2,525; QuantumDigital 46¢ (8×6.5) to 58¢ (11×8.5) per piece at 5,000, postage included |
| A: print and postage at threshold | $2,300–2,800 | Clubflyers $743.99 print + USPS $0.26 per piece, or M13 full service $120 + $0.262 per piece plus print |

| Result | Gross collected | Net (after print, postage, B cost, caller and tools) |
|---|---|---|
| p10 | $0 | −$1,500 (hourly caller) / −$400 (commission caller) |
| p50 | **$2,935** | −$890 / −$218 |
| p90 | **$13,568** | +$2,286 / +$1,958 |
| ≥ $10k | **16.7% of worlds** | |
| ≥ $5k | 41.9% of worlds | |

**What the numbers say:**

1. **Gross is not profit.** Line B is mostly pass-through (about 15% margin).
   $10k gross nets about $2–4k. If "revenue" means *net*, this plan does not
   reach it, and nothing on file does.
2. **The plan's value is the upper tail plus cheap learning.** Line B carries it
   (mean $3.7k against A's $1.6k), and B's interest rate is the least-known
   input. The day-7 gate exists to measure it.
3. **Do not split Zone 1 and Zone 2.** In the model, selling both zones at once
   cut Zone 1's chance of reaching its threshold from 55% to 11%. Thresholds
   punish spreading thin. Zone 2 opens only after Zone 1 passes its threshold.

## 5. Buyers and customer acquisition

### Line A: Neighbor Card, Zone 1

**Who buys.** Businesses serving Camp Hill and East Hampden: fitness, health,
dental, home improvement, HVAC, real estate, insurance, restaurants. One per
category.

**How they are reached:**

1. **Within 24 hours, answer the 5 interested buyers.** Send the two-sided
   mock-up PDF and the rate card. Answer West Shire's demographic question with
   Census figures for 17011. Include a payment link and two dates: the
   category-exclusivity rule and the threshold date.
2. **Keep emailing.** The hourly Neighbor Card workflow keeps refilling
   (Mechanicsburg Chamber, OpenStreetMap, MX-checked). Widen the radius to
   Harrisburg, Lemoyne and Mechanicsburg businesses that serve 17011.
3. **Phone.** A hired caller works the 80 phone-only prospects in
   `prospects.csv` and every email non-responder, three passes each.
   - The script is `SALES-KIT.md`.
   - **Manual dialing only.** No autodialer or prerecorded calls, because of
     the TCPA rules on calls to mobile numbers. Honour every do-not-call
     request.
   - The caller sends the payment link while still on the call.

### Line B: January EDDM, done for you

**Who buys.** Independent local-service businesses with a January season,
nationally, split evenly across four segments:

- independent tax preparers;
- HVAC and plumbing;
- dentists;
- gyms and studios.

Replies are read by segment, and volume moves to whichever segment wins.

**The offer.** "Your postcard in about 5,000 homes in your service area the
week of Jan 11. Design, routes, print and postage included. **$2,750 prepaid.**
Reserve by Dec 18."

**Channel.** Brain's draft campaign `Brain · buyer outreach` (`4499afed…`),
150–600 sends a day, MX-checked lists, CAN-SPAM footer (address plus opt-out).

### Closing, both lines

- An interested reply gets an AI-drafted answer within 4 business hours: mock-up,
  price, payment link.
- The caller phones every interested lead the same day.
- The owner approves nothing per message. Brain's standing sending rules (§94)
  apply.

## 10. Challenge: where this plan could be wrong

- **"5 interested" may only mean curiosity.** "Send an example" costs nothing to
  write, and no close has ever happened in this workspace. Gate 1 tests this
  before any real spend.
- **The upper tail rests on Line B**, whose interest input is borrowed from a
  different offer (AI Dispatch). It could be better: postcards are concrete, and
  the co-op got 4× AI Dispatch's interest. It could be worse: $2,750 is 9× the
  co-op ticket. Nobody knows until about 1,000 leads have been sent.
- **Collected is not kept.** At p90, net is about $2.3k. If the owner's real
  objective is profit, this plan is a learning investment, not a $10k outcome.
- **What would genuinely move the odds**, none of it assumed here:
  - a warm network of local business owners;
  - the owner taking closing calls (outside "minimal involvement");
  - a repeat edition in February from renewals (outside the 30 days).
