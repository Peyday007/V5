LIMITS (hard rules):
- The money still needed: $500 (USD), collected within 7 day(s).
- Capital: at most $0 may be tied up before money comes back. A route needing more is not allowed.
- Owner's time: at most 15 hour(s) of the owner's own work in the whole window. Routes must fit inside it.
- Time: the first money must settle inside 7 day(s). A buyer who pays in arrears, on invoice terms or after a long sales cycle that would land later is not allowed.
- No industry is excluded: choose on economics, time to cash and whether it can be done.
- This operation is run entirely online, with AI and software doing as much of the work as possible: the owner does no physical work — no travel, no deliveries, no distributing anything, no meeting customers, no performing a service and no running a physical operation. Work that is physical is still in scope when an independent supplier or subcontractor performs it and this operation coordinates it remotely, as a broker, referrer or subcontracting coordinator. Look in every country and region: where the buyers are is a business choice made on demand, margin, fulfilment access, how fast money settles, competition and how much can be automated — never on where the owner lives.
- For this goal only, the owner offered their own work ("I can put in 15 hours"): routes the owner personally performs — in person included — are allowed, using only the skills and assets their words state and inside their stated hours. Set performer OWNER and count every one of those hours.

FEASIBILITY (every route must pass every one):
- uses what the owner said they have — skills, assets, location, free time — or a priced subcontractor; never expertise the owner did not claim, and never a menu that would fit any request; when the owner states a licence, certification or trade, at least one route sells that licensed or trade work itself to the buyers who pay for it, rather than only products around it

- the capital is read as the owner gave it: a small budget rules out spend-heavy channels; a large one may fund inventory, equipment or paid labour where that is what earns the money; few owner hours rule out labour-per-unit work that does not scale; a recurring target needs recurring billing
