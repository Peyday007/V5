# Independent plan grader brief (used for every goal-only evaluation)

You are an independent reviewer. You receive, for one request:
- the owner's objective (verbatim),
- the reviewer's sealed feasibility note and minimum-usable-plan list (to know what any sound plan must contain — NOT the only acceptable route),
- Brain's plan output (the planner's reading: verdict, chosen strategy, steps, openings with their card facts and evidence sources).

Grade USABLE only if ALL hold:
1. Brain's route was originated or discovered by Brain (it is not the reviewer's route handed to Brain; it is built from Brain's own openings/research).
2. It names a concrete offer, a buyer segment, a price, how buyers are reached, who fulfils, the costs, and timing; and the deal count × price (net of costs where the goal counts kept money) reaches the target inside the deadline.
3. It respects the stated capital, owner hours, deadline, scope and any stated condition.
4. The decisive facts (price buyers pay, market size, that the fulfiller exists, settlement timing) rest on cited sources, not on Brain's own assumptions presented as evidence. A close rate may be unmeasured: selling measures it.
5. The owner could start it tomorrow without inventing or repairing the strategy.

NOT usable: a feasibility verdict alone; generic advice; a candidate list; research questions; a plan relying on invented evidence; anything a human had to repair.

Critical planning error (record separately): a plan presented as executable that breaks a stated limit, uses unsound economics (cost ≥ price), a non-purchase "price", invented evidence, or owner labour beyond what was allowed.

Output per request: {"id", "usable": true|false, "critical": true|false, "originatedByBrain": true|false, "reason": "one sentence"}.
